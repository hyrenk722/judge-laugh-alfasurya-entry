Entry for the make.fun competition "Make the AI Judge Laugh": one joke about
office life, a single line under 200 characters. `main.py` writes that joke
to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the
gate first: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one
line — then reading the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> I told my manager I'm drowning in emails, so he CC'd me on a meeting about email overload.

Why this joke: it is true to the office twice over — drowning in emails and
being CC'd on meetings nobody asked for are both things every office worker
recognises — and the turn is the cure becoming the disease: the manager's
answer to email overload is one more email, plus a meeting about it. One
line, no wasted setup, reads cleanly aloud in one breath, and the last word,
"overload", lands the laugh instead of explaining it.
